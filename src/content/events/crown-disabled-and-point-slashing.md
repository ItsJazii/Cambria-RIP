Posted to the Cambria Discord #updates channel in the wake of the emerald reversal:

- T1 Emeralds fixed; the Crown mechanic disabled.
- Cloudflare Turnstile checks no longer expire while you are AFK.
- F2P slots per spot reduced to 5.
- Fishing Sack base RTP corrected to range 65-98% max, having "previously silently got close to 100%".
- Fishing Points up 20%, no 3x multiplier on clawed-back T1 points, and points slashed for any coordinated or botted T1 farming in the past week.

The team also addressed the rumours directly: the high-T4 streaks players had noticed were "a rig-loading bug, not an exploit or an economy drain" - every rig pays the same over time, so it changed which tiers landed on first casts rather than creating free value.
