v0.2.0's headline was Demonic Power: a resource that gates your descent to the next floor, where every floor down "MASSIVELY increases both the chance of failure as well as the potential payout and rewards."

Two lines below the headline, Cursed Pact and Chest Keys were disabled pending rework. Features in Cambria are rarely deleted; they are quarantined.
