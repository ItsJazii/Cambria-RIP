Cores is Cambria's second NFT collection: 10,000 factory NFTs minting December 20th, 2024, which let holders directly own the issuance of new in-game Companions ("pets"), Skins, Lootboxes and other high-demand items.

The pitch is player-owned supply instead of a studio store. The consequence is that the people who mint the items and the people who need the items are now two different constituencies with two different incentives - a tension that shows up in nearly every balance patch that follows.
