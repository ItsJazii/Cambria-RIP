The April 2026 dev update is mostly a roadmap post: major changes to networking, the game tick and surrounding architecture shipped and tested live, with a promise that "feel should be significantly tighter."

Further down, the backend scaling section confirms what players had been reporting for months: Islands (3,333 instances) and dynamically scaling Dungeon instances are now integrated, and "crash fixes and legacy issues with memory leaks on Islands have been resolved."
