Buried in the Season 2 disclaimers: "If we do not catch a item duplication exploit in time to mitigate its impact, we may roll back the game economy to a previous point in time." Anomalous economic activity is monitored in real time; on detection, the simulation is paused pending investigation.

Most MMOs have a rollback button. Most MMOs are not settling that state against withdrawals denominated in ETH. Fifteen months later, this clause stops being theoretical.
