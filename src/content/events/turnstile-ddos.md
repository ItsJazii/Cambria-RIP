Three items from one patch tell the story of a game outgrowing its plumbing: Turnstile added against DDoS and flooding attacks, an early Lightning Client build for performance "especially in regions where there are hundreds of player and npc entities moving around at once", and a fix for fishing spots that had been inaccessible.

Two weeks later, that same Turnstile check would be expiring on AFK players mid-session and need its own fix.
