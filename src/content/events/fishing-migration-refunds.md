From the patch note: players who fished the day before were migrated to the new Mirelle's Favor system based on points, bait usage and progression, and Dungeon Key refunds were issued broadly to players shortchanged at low Fishing levels, under the posted rates. Check your mailbox if you were affected.

The mechanism worked. The uncomfortable part is that the rates a player is shown and the rates the server actually pays are separate numbers that can drift - which is precisely the failure that arrives three weeks later, in the other direction.
