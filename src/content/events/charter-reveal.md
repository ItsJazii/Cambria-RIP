Traits were rolled after the mint: Voyage, Standing, House, Home City, Affinity, Passage and Race. Per the founder's own rarity summary, 3% rolled no Affinity, 2% rolled all five, and about 5% rolled no House (Freeborn) or no Home City (Undocumented).

The market split hard by tier. Viceroy holders posted "0.06 mint → 0.4 ETH floor". By October 26, Founding Citizen Charters, the entry tier, were trading at 0.05 ETH, under their mint price, and Viceroys had cooled to 0.29 ETH.

Trait utility is still mostly listed as "Flavor / Lore / ???", and Standing "is still early in design, with few details shared to date".
