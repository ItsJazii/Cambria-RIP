Genesis merges Dungeons, Islands and Gold Rush into one world that never resets. It has five major cities, buildings you can buy and tax, and guild treasuries. Founder Ben.zzz called it "the first nation-scale game economy" and warned of "upfront risk": "None of this will be a free lunch that you can just freely take profit from."

Entry is gated. A "supply-capped group of initial citizens" goes first through an invite-only whitelist, and past players had to accept a Genesis Event Opt-In Agreement by September 29 at 11pm ET.

The new Cambria Foundation said $RSGP will "form the core of ecosystem consensus mechanisms". It did not give a supply, distribution, chain or date. The presale in March had estimated June 2026 for the TGE.
