Total supply 5,000,000,000 RSGP on Base (CA `0x44B63aF715D5ef02CC3646a6CE96875e11d69E85`), with roughly 25% circulating at TGE:

- **15%** Genesis airdrop, 100% unlocked.
- **5.2%** presale, also fully unlocked.
- **5%** liquidity, seeded into the RSGP/USDC pool on Aerodrome.

The docs still listed centralized exchanges as "TBA" while MEXC was already advertising RSGP/USDT for 15:00 UTC the next day, plus an Airdrop+ pool of 25M RSGP and 10,000 USDT for its users. The docs also warned that fake RSGP tokens would show up at launch, so check the contract address, not the name.
