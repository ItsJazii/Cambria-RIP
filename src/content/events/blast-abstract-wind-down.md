The PSA: "Game functionality will be migrated off of the Blast and Abstract chains on October 19th at 11am ET. Game treasuries and unclaimed Duel Arena stakes on these chains will be moved to @base and @ethereum mainnet as part of the migration to prevent loss."

Duel Arena processed over $140M in lifetime volume. Its leftover stakes - winnings never withdrawn, and refunds from voided duels - had sat in the Blast and Abstract escrows for 12+ months. On October 19 they were snapshotted, withdrawn and bridged to the Cambria treasury. After that, the only way to get yours back is to find your wallet in the published snapshot and open a ticket in Discord.

Spend tokens move to Base USDC and Robinhood USDG. Accounts with only AGW + Privy linked were told to add another login before Abstract goes dark on December 15, or risk losing their way into the wallet.

It is the right call to protect the funds. It is also one more Cambria payout that now needs a support ticket.
