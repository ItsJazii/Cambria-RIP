Over 10 days, players and guilds spent $1.7 million USD on Charters and Energy Orbs. The main pot came to 401.36 ETH and 2,692,908 RON from 24,523 Charter purchases and 233,850 Orb purchases, with 316.27 ETH of it flowing back through a 41.5 billion Silver in-game supply at roughly $31 per million Silver.

The bug bounty line is the interesting one: no "Critical" severity exploits were reported this season, so a fixed 3.2 ETH was split between 13 players who reported lower-severity issues, and the remainder was pushed back into the Creator Program.
