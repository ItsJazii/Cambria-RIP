v0.4 rebuilt The Depths around survival and added Necromancy and boosted Cut Gem runs. The fix list for the follow-up v0.4.1 is the interesting part:

- **The one-shot bug.** "Enemy damage and accuracy were scaling with the *square* of the floor, while your health only grew linearly."
- **The hidden linger penalty.** "Artifact value used to quietly decay the longer you spent on a floor, with nothing on screen telling you." It was removed outright.
- **Whirlwind** "was hitting a 2x2 area instead of the 3x3 on its card," and its auto-cast range was stricter than the spin itself.
- The Crossbow, "the most accurate weapon in the game **and** the highest damage," had its bolt strength cut by about 15%.

Players who were losing value on every floor had no way to see it on screen. They were paying a cost the game never showed them.

Points earned after the Emerald fix but before this update were promised a manual upward adjustment in a follow-up pass.
