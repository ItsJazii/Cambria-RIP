Islands is a collection of 3,333 NFTs on Ethereum granting holders a writ of dominion to an island off the shore of New Cambria. Holders host "Islets" for settlers, and take a cut of what those settlers produce.

It is a clean economic loop and an ugly infrastructure bill: 3,333 persistent instances that have to be spawned, kept warm and torn down. The memory leaks show up in the changelog four months later.
