The docs state that over $85k USD has been paid in bug bounties to date, covering the Lobby, the game frontend and Gold Rush, with rewards weighted mostly on potential economic impact and funds at risk.

The terms are blunt: reporting does not guarantee a reward, exploiting the bug before reporting voids eligibility, and sharing or selling an exploit earns a permanent ban plus ineligibility for both prize pool and bounty. The same page notes that Cambria's permissionless setup limits what support can actually do for you.
