Cosmetic Shards ("pink shards") went live as a rare soulbound resource earned by staking Cambria Core NFTs, spendable for a split of in-game Arena Token lootbox spend or used to bid on cosmetics.

Counting along at home: Silver, Keys, Arena Tokens, Energy Orbs, Royal Favor, Trinkets, Glimmer, Scales and now Shards. Each one is a surface where an exchange rate can be wrong.
