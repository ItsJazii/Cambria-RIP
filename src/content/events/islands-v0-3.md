The v0.3 Islands patch normalised energy costs across extraction skills (200/400/800/1600 by tier), tied rare drop chances to energy consumed, and made woodcutting a flat 20 energy with no rare rolls.

The revealing line is the nerf: the Founder/Island boost on Energy Orbs was lowered to a maximum 8% "because the boosted Rare Drops (esp. Relic) RTP is so sensitive, these would result in essentially free AT if left at previous values." A stacked multiplier had been quietly printing money.
