v0.5.1, the "Build Power Update", rewrote the numbers on almost every build. Whirlwind went from 150%/230% to 450%/690% per tick, Meteor Strike from 215%/335% to 645%/1005%, and defensive picks rose across the board.

It also fixed "two long-standing bugs". Kill-triggered ailment effects had been reading the enemy as already cured, which meant quirks like **Venom Bloom** never spread poison on kill as described.

At the bottom of the patch: "Necromancy is initially disabled until we can collect more data from the latest pass." The new build that headlined v0.4 on August 21 was on the bench nine days later.
