Season 1 was the first time Cambria let players put real money behind a RuneScape-shaped grind. Over five days, 9,600+ players minted 5,340 Royal Charters and bought 33,699 Energy Orbs, contributing $209,300 to a player-funded prize pool.

Nothing broke badly enough to make headlines. The important part is the shape of the thing: an MMO economy where every mined rock, every fishing spot and every death maps onto a number that a payout contract will eventually honour. Once that is true, a rounding error stops being a rounding error.
