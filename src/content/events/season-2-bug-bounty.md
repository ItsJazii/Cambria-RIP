Over two weeks, players and guilds spent $1.3 million USD on Charters and Energy Orbs. Out of that pool, 35.76 ETH - about $65,000 - went to the 20 players and security researchers who were first to report critical exploits, dupes or security issues to the team.

Cambria frames the scaling bounty as a feature: the bigger the pot, the more it pays to be a white hat rather than a very quiet millionaire. It is a good policy. It is also an admission that, in a live risk-to-earn economy, twenty critical holes is a normal fortnight.
