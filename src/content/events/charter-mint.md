There are 512 invite-only Viceroys and 3,584 allowlist Citizens. Allowlist spots were scored on Cambria history and on wallet activity on other chains. Holders unlock advanced zones, NFT loot and Royal Favor, which converts into $RSGP, but only after staking the Charter to an in-game Venue.

The collection sold out on October 8, putting about $570,000 into the Crown Treasury. By October 10, cumulative resale volume was $236,000, and the Citizen floor had "plunged" about 63% to roughly $155 while Viceroys held around $930.

Charter perks were still "yet to be fully detailed" at mint, and the $RSGP chain had not been chosen.
