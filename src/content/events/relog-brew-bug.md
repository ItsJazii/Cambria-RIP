Tucked under the Fisherman's Badges event, the Dungeon Fishing notes contain one line: "Bug where relogging allowed you to Brew / Doubleshot more fixed."

Brew stacking was one of the ingredients in the August 10 emerald over-emission. Three weeks later, a relog was still enough to get more boosted casts than the game intended.

The same patch raised the Brew house cut "to reduce its edge on uplift".
