Attached to the Season 3 results was a follow-up list that reads like an incident log:

- Raw logs were being trawled to recover Bribes lost by players who missed a 24-hour claim window that predated the announcement and the UI warning.
- Accounts created with a social sign-in (Google, Discord) could still see "No Rewards Found" on the rewards site.
- Players who had significantly over-stockpiled Energy were promised an additional Trinket boost.
- Guild and Paymaster silver distribution edge cases were still being worked through as individual tickets.

Every one of those is a person who did the grind and then had to open a support ticket to find out what they won.
