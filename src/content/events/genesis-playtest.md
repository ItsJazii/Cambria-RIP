From the founder: "thank you to all the charter holders who came out to our quick playtest of @playcambria Genesis today! incredible amount of moving parts w/ mainnet so getting to this first step + data from hundreds of live testers is big for us ahead of broader testnet golive. big week ahead"

The groundwork shipped in v0.6.2 on September 28: Genesis Client v0, a performance rework, and fixes for ghost NPCs, tilemap wobble and an item-drag freeze that "sometimes" broke all interactions. Earlier, the plan was a $RSGP launch first and an incentivized testnet with a bug bounty after. That testnet is still the next step.
