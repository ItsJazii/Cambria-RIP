$RSGP was announced as the ownership layer of Cambria's economy - treasury governance, rewards, ve-style staking for fee reduction, guild advantages, polling and cosmetics - with Loot Chests airdropped ahead of the August TGE to players who "stuck around in the bear market."

Coverage highlighted the part players tend to discover late: a strict 14-day claim window. Seven months earlier, Cambria was combing raw logs to recover Bribes that players lost to a 24-hour claim window.
