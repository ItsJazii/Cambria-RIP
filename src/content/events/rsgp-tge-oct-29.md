The timeline of a token "coming in August":

- **March 2026:** presale at a $30M FDV, TGE estimated for June.
- **July 22:** "$RSGP is coming" with an August TGE and a 14-day Loot Chest claim window.
- **October 12:** Base picked as the home chain via an Aerodrome "Cleared for landing" post. Community chatter pointed to October 13-14.
- **October 26:** Thursday October 29 confirmed, after three earlier windows slipped.

A day before, Cambria launched "Your Cambria Journey", a dashboard that totals each player's seasonal points, loot chests, NFTs and renown into an allocation. Players with Mirelle's Adjustments from the August emerald clean-up have those deducted from their airdrop at launch.
