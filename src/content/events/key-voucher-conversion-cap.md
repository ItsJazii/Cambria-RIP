The vouchers handed out after the emerald reversal finally got a use. Holders can now pick one of two paths: sell vouchers back to Keys, or hold them and earn roughly 128.65 Points per voucher per day.

The catch is in the hint box. The conversion limit "is based on the USDC currently in the pool, with the shortfall from the Aug 10th Emerald bug slowly covered with Key sinks from game fees." Put plainly, the pool does not yet hold enough USDC to honour every voucher, so redemptions are rationed while fees fill the gap.

The team says it is "exploring other options to sink more keys or add more USDC to the pool." Cashing out is permanent, and vouchers you cash out stop earning yield.
